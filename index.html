Our Story

Small Farm. Real Food. Real People.

We're a family farm rooted in Raymond, Nebraska — growing food with care, raising hens on pasture, and building real relationships with our community.

🏡

Who We Are

Sakinah Ridge Farm LLC is a small family-run operation in Raymond, NE. We believe food grown close to home — with honesty and intention — nourishes in ways that go beyond nutrition.

🐔

How We Raise Our Hens

Our hens live on open pasture with room to roam, scratch, and forage. No crowded cages — just happy chickens producing the kind of eggs you can taste the difference in.

🥚

Why Our Eggs Are Different

Pasture-raised eggs from hens that move freely have richer yolks and better flavor. Collected fresh and delivered or picked up locally — you know exactly where your food comes from.
