Our Story

Our Story

Sakinah Ridge Farm began as a small family project rooted in faith, stewardship, and community. We started with a handful of hens and a simple goal: provide fresh, honest food to neighbors in and around Raymond. Today, we remain committed to careful farming, healthy animals, and relationships built on trust.

How We Raise Our Hens

How We Raise Our Hens

🌿

Free-Range / Pasture Access

Our hens have daily access to pasture where they can roam, scratch, and forage naturally.

💧

Clean Feed & Fresh Water

We provide quality feed and constant clean water to keep our flock healthy and productive.

🤍

Humane, Stress-Free Environment

Low-stress care and clean living space help support stronger birds and better eggs.
